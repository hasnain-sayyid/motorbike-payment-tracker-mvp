📱 HOW TO TEST SMS:

1. OPEN YOUR BROWSER: http://localhost:3000

   - Enter YOUR phone number (the one you want to test)
   - Verify it with the code Twilio sends

3. TEST SMS:
   - Click the purple "📱 Test SMS" button
   - Enter your VERIFIED phone number (e.g., +919876543210)
   - Enter a test message
   - You'll receive a REAL SMS!
